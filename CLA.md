gives us the permissions we need to keep the project both open source and sustainable.

## How to sign

- **Automated (preferred):** Our CLA bot will comment on your first pull request with a
  one-click sign-off. Once you accept, you're set for all future contributions.
- **Manual:** If asked, reply to your PR with:
  > I have read the CLA and I hereby sign it.
  and include your name and GitHub username.
